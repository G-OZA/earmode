EarMode Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

EarMode is developed by Koinon.

Short version: EarMode does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It works entirely on your phone and has no internet access.

What EarMode does

EarMode routes the sound of other apps to your phone's earpiece while you hold the phone to your ear, and turns the screen off, like during a phone call. Everything happens locally on your device.

Data we collect

None. EarMode has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Sensors and permissions

  • Proximity sensor: used only to detect when the phone is at your ear. Readings are processed instantly on the device and never saved.
  • Audio settings: used to switch the output between earpiece and speaker and to keep call and media volume in sync.
  • Notifications and foreground service: used to show that ear mode is running and to let you stop it.
  • Wake lock: keeps the proximity sensor working while the screen is off.

EarMode does not access your microphone, contacts, location, files or the content of what you listen to.

Diagnostics

The app keeps a short technical log (for example "proximity near, switched to earpiece") in memory while it runs, to help with troubleshooting. It is deleted when the app stops. It only leaves the app if you tap "Copy diagnostics" and paste it somewhere yourself.

Children

EarMode does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
